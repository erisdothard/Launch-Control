04 — Colour

Carbon#050505 · ground
Board#0D0D0F · plates
Volt#C8FF00 · the message
Chalk#F2F2EF · type, light ground

Volt is the message, never the ground. It is used for the turn in a headline, the number on a board, the one button that matters. If a page is more than 10% volt, something is wrong. Grey #8A8F98 for labels. Red #FF2D2D exists for one word only: an error.

05 — Voice

The pit wall doesn't hype. It tells the driver what's happening and what to do next. Short. Specific. Numbers over adjectives. Second person. Full stops.

Not this

Unlock exponential growth with our cutting-edge, end-to-end business development solutions.

You've got the car. We're the pit wall.

Not this

Our team of passionate experts leverages best-in-class tools.
